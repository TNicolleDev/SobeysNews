"Population Growing": Warehouse Scab Numbers Surge as Striking Workers Turn to Creative Side Hustles

As replacement worker counts reach record highs at the facility, Teamsters Local 419 members are resorting to makeshift fundraisers on the picket line to make ends meet.

Staff Report Filed Today · 4 min read

Tensions outside the warehouse gates reached a new peak this week as the headcount of replacement workers continuing to cross the picket line hit an all-time high. Striking employees have taken notice, marking the growing tally on makeshift signs stationed along the roadside.

With strike pay stretched thin and monthly household bills piling up, members of Teamsters Local 419 are having to get creative just to stay afloat while holding the line.

"The headcount inside keeps climbing, but so does our resolve. If we have to sell dollar kisses and Hershey's Kisses on the sidewalk to pay our bills, that's exactly what we'll do." — Striking Warehouse Worker, Teamsters Local 419

Opposite the growing "Scabville" headcounts, strikers have set up makeshift booths along the perimeter sidewalk. Among the most notable additions is a hastily constructed plywood and tarp "Kissing Booth," where workers are accepting small donations and selling chocolate treats for charity and personal relief funds.

While management maintains that warehouse operations remain on track with replacement staff, union members say the humorous and desperate measures outside highlight the real economic strain being placed on long-term workers demanding fair pay.

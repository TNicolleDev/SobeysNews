"Population Growing": Warehouse Scab Numbers Surge as Striking Workers Turn to Creative Side Hustles

As replacement worker counts reached record highs at the facility in December 2023, Teamsters Local 419 members resorted to makeshift fundraisers on the picket line to make ends meet.

Staff Report Filed December 2023 · 4 min read

In December 2023, tensions outside the warehouse gates reached a new peak as the headcount of replacement workers continuing to cross the picket line hit an all-time high. Striking employees marked the growing tally on makeshift signs stationed along the roadside.

With strike pay stretched thin and monthly household bills piling up, members of Teamsters Local 419 had to get creative just to stay afloat while holding the line.

"The headcount inside keeps climbing, but so does our resolve. If we have to sell dollar kisses and Hershey's Kisses on the sidewalk to pay our bills, that's exactly what we'll do." — Striking Warehouse Worker, Teamsters Local 419

Opposite the growing "Scabville" headcounts, strikers set up makeshift booths along the perimeter sidewalk during the December 2023 labor action. Among the most notable additions was a hastily constructed plywood and tarp "Kissing Booth," where workers accepted small donations and sold chocolate treats for charity and personal relief funds.

At the time, management maintained that warehouse operations remained on track with replacement staff, while union members said the humorous yet desperate measures outside highlighted the real economic strain facing long-term workers demanding fair pay.
